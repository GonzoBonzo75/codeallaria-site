Basic training

Generally, when people approach an education course, they think of the dog learning to respond to commands (and that will certainly happen). What we don't take into account, though, is a very fortunate side effect: there will be many more things that he teaches us. Yes, because working with a dog is a journey inside ourselves, into our limits, our fears and our preconceptions. Very soon you will discover that the dog is never wrong. If he fails an exercise, it is because we were indecisive, inconsistent or unclear, we expected too much too soon, we didn't trust him, we weren't able to listen to him, and so on. That is why, for us, education means above all building a relationship. In dog training there are no magic, universal formulas that can solve any issue in the time of a TikTok. At the root, you need to create a deep bond and mutual understanding with the dog. We must become, for him, that creature who is interesting above all else, who makes him feel safe, understood, respected in his needs and relieved of responsibilities that (for his well-being) he should not take on. A fun creature who knows how to engage him and with whom, as a result, he will want to collaborate. Once these foundations are laid, the most common problems ("pulls on the leash", "doesn't come back when called", "can't be left alone" and others) become manageable. The first meeting we propose is at your home, to assess the dog, the management of his daily routine and of the various resources: food, spaces, toys (play is extremely important). Right after, we ask together with you what you want to achieve as a pair. Goals, like problems, are not the same for everyone: the program we propose is therefore tailored to you. We work both in the field and in the city, so that the dog learns in the presence of the different distractions that everyday life offers. In this way, correct behaviors will become consolidated, always through the method of positive reinforcement. Above all, a long, happy, genuine friendship will be consolidated.

Guided socialization

We know it: in the city, opportunities to let your dog run free and interact safely with other dogs are scarce. Dog parks are often crowded and unhygienic, and owners are not always able to handle group dynamics properly, which can turn into a negative experience for your puppy. Code all'Aria offers, for a limited number of participants and weekly hours, guided socialization in our fenced, hazard-free field. Our packs are small and strictly compatible, to guarantee your dog the best possible experience: one that builds confidence, security and new skills.

Home boarding

We thought of a real holiday for your friend, just when you are going away too. With our home boarding, he will feel right at home. The rules, habits and diet stay the ones you have set for him. No cages, no improvisation, but games, cuddles and walks in the green. Behind it, there is careful work to get to know him and a gradual introduction, so that the dog is perfectly at ease with the home and the family hosting him. You will be kept constantly informed about his adventures. The only downside? Places are limited, but that is exactly what allows us to guarantee a careful service.
