Our story

Code all’Aria was born from our meeting: the meeting between Barbara, a CSEN dog educator who also works as an educator in the field of disability, and Ombretta, an ENCI trainer with a background in sales. Combining our skills and aptitudes, in 2021 we created a Dog Playground that, for five years, gave us affection and satisfaction. We invested a great deal of time and energy in it, with effort, passion and a touch of recklessness.

At a certain point it became clear that our creature had to grow, and we asked ourselves how. A project was proposed to us from outside that turned out not to be the right one. It wasn’t easy. We talked things through, we clashed and, in the end, we understood that in order to move forward we had to start again from ourselves. And so it was: the new Code all’Aria could not resemble us more. It is not just a story of friendship: it is the fusion of our respective human and professional journeys.

The playground and home boarding keep an important but secondary role. The focus is on education. From puppy classes to educational walks, from individual classes for adults to wedding dog sitting, our programs are not presented as standard: they are tailored to the needs of each dog and handler. They take place both in the field and in the urban setting, so that you can face different stimuli and challenges. Then there is scent work (our passion, for how rewarding it is and how it engages dogs mentally). This activity, too, is tailored to you, whether it takes place in the city or in the woods. It is an education that above all aims to build a relationship within the dog-handler pair and is able to follow the dog through every stage of its life. Last but not least, our collaborations in the fields of childhood and disability.

This is Code all’Aria. Thank you to those who choose, and to those who will choose, to entrust us with their most faithful friend. Our dream has grown with us, but one thing doesn’t change: seeing them happy is our one obsession.
